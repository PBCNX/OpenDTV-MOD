User-supplied vendor images
===========================

This build contains no vendor images.

The two USB tuner drivers it does build - the IT9175 (048d:275a) and the IT9308 (048d:9308) - drive a
bridge chip that starts blank: a vendor image must be uploaded into it every time the stick is plugged
in. Those images belong to the chip and bridge vendors. They are not distributed with this source or
this app, and nothing here grants any right to them.

The app therefore asks you to supply the image once, in Settings -> Tuner setup. It is copied into the
app's private storage, checked against the SHA-256 of the image the driver was written for, and
refused if it is not byte-for-byte that file. Nothing is uploaded to a stick before that check passes.

What this notice does NOT claim:
  - no right to the vendor images, express or implied;
  - no permission to modify or redistribute them;
  - no statement that the vendor licenses their redistribution.

It records only what the software expects to be handed, and what it refuses.

The images, and the hashes the app checks:
  it9175-ite-v3046.bin     9,036 bytes   sha256 451b09a4...c3575c   ITE IT9175
  it9308-sony-1.10.bin     6,498 bytes   sha256 e4e94633...4704a70  IT930x bridge (Sony 1.10)

The full hashes are in DRIVERS.md at the root of this repository.
