Sony 9308 / Endeavour - IT9300 USB tuner support

The Sony/IT9300 ("Endeavour") tuner path links three vendor libraries:

    libendeavour-sony-jni.so
    libendeavour-sonytuner.so
    libsonytuner.so

They are the vendor's own binaries, redistributed only in the private build so the dongle can be
driven on the bench and by testers. They are not open source, and the MIT licence of OpenDTV-MOD
does not cover them. The public source tree contains none of them.
